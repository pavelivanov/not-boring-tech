1Filters out of the sidebar — three popover systems

The sidebar spent a full column on controls used a few times per session, and pushed the results — the actual product — into a narrow gutter. In all three the panel is summoned, sticky while you work (it never closes on selection or mouse-out; only the trigger, ✕, Done or Esc dismiss it), and whatever you picked stays legible on the page afterwards. All three are live — click the triggers.
