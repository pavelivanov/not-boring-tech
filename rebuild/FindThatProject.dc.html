1a The Ledger

Rows instead of cards. Two jobs are split into two modes: Unseen answers "what happened since I was here," Index answers "find me a thing." Hierarchy is derived, not written: the highest-traction entry gets the lead slot, star magnitude is a bar, and each row carries one machine-generated reason it's worth a look. Clicking a row marks it seen — reading empties the queue.
